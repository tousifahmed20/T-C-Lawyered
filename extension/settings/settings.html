🆓 Free setup — no credit card needed

T&C Lawyered runs on your own AI key so nothing is ever billed to us, and you don’t need to spend anything either. OpenRouter gives you a free key with access to capable :free models. Takes about two minutes:

  1. Go to openrouter.ai and sign up (Google or GitHub — free, no card).
  2. Open openrouter.ai/settings/keys and click Create Key. Copy the key (starts with sk-or-).
  3. Paste it into the openrouter card below and pick a :free model.
  4. Click Save, then Test to confirm it works.
  5. Click Use this to make OpenRouter your active provider. Done — start scanning policies.

:free models cost $0 but are rate-limited and may change — see the current free list. Prefer another provider? Add Anthropic, OpenAI, or Gemini below instead.
